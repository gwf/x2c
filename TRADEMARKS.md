# x2c trademark policy

This is the trademark policy for the x2c project. It is separate from
the Apache License 2.0, which licenses software and documentation but does not
grant general permission to use a licensor's trade names, trademarks, service
marks, or product names.

## Project identifiers

The x2c name, official logo, and wordmark are trademarks of Gary William
Flake. They identify the official project and its releases. Gary William
Flake decides which software, services, publications, and events are
authorized to present themselves as official x2c offerings.

This policy does not claim that any mark is federally registered.

## Uses that do not require project approval

Subject to applicable trademark law, no project approval is required to:

- refer accurately to the official x2c project or software;
- state truthfully that software is written in, works with, or is compatible
  with x2c;
- reproduce unmodified x2c software while preserving its origin and license;
  or
- state that a fork or other work is based on x2c, provided the statement
  does not imply official status, sponsorship, or endorsement.

Use only as much of the name or logo as is reasonably necessary for the
reference. Do not imply that an independent product or service comes from, is
approved by, or is supported by the official x2c project.

## Modified distributions and forks

Modified distributions may describe their relationship to x2c, but must use
a distinct primary product name and branding unless the Project Lead gives
written permission. They must not identify themselves as an official x2c
release or use confusingly similar branding.

## Permission and questions

Direct trademark permission requests and questions to Gary William Flake
at [trademark@flake.org](mailto:trademark@flake.org). Describe
the proposed use and include a link or example of the branding where possible.
