title: Code is Love
lede: "The part you touch all day should feel good in the hand."
---

This is the first idea. Every rough edge of C is gone, and nothing good is
lost. Your headers, your structs, your build, and your debugger are all
still there, and so is the speed. What you get back is the list you used
to need an interpreter for: one value type, text and collections with
methods, interpolation, destructuring, lambdas, pattern matching, and
memory freed by scope. You get to have your cake and eat it.

You do not have to marry x2c, either. Every feature is optional, so you
can date it one file at a time and keep the rest of your project as it
was. Nobody ever fell in love with boilerplate. Twelve examples, each one
small.
