title: Code is Power
lede: "The second idea: pieces that compound."
---

Classes, protocols, derived methods, and packages build on one another.
Threads and channels, errors as data, and HTTP come with the runtime. A
server is 230 lines. In the
[Benchmarks Game suite](https://github.com/gwf/x2c/blob/main/examples/shootout/README.md),
idiomatic x2c ran at a median of 2.49 times C's time and tuned x2c at 1.66;
Python took 20 times. Seven examples.
