Classes, protocols, derived methods, and packages build on one another.
Threads and channels, errors as data, and HTTP come with the runtime. A
server is 230 lines. In the
[1 November 2026 Benchmarks Game run](https://github.com/gwf/x2c/blob/f5031f767e3905e650af9ae2d9733182e45c1d3f/examples/shootout/results/current.md),
idiomatic x2c ran at a median of 2.52 times C's time and tuned x2c at 1.07;
Python took 20 times. Seven examples.
