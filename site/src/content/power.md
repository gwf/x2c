Classes, protocols, derived methods, and packages build on one another.

server is 209 lines. In the
[language shootout](https://github.com/gwf/x2c/blob/main/examples/shootout/README.md),
fourteen problems ran at a median of 1.04 times C's time; Python took 39
times. Seven examples.
