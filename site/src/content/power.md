This is the second idea. Once the pieces are simple, they fit together,
and the things C makes hard become a few lines each: pattern matching,
lambdas, threads and channels, jobs, files, HTTP, your own classes and
protocols, a C library imported under a name you choose. The notes
service on this page is 209 lines, and it was measured against Go.

That is the superpower. What you would reach for Python to do, you can do
in x2c in about the same number of lines, and it runs at C speed. In the
[language shootout](https://github.com/gwf/x2c/blob/main/examples/shootout/README.md),
fourteen problems written in x2c ran at a median of 1.04 times C's time,
and Python took 39 times. Links against everything you already have.
