# x2c support

x2c is experimental and is provided without a support commitment or service
level agreement. The book at https://x2c-lang.dev/docs/ is the public
support resource.

Bug reports, ideas, and pull requests are welcome under
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md). Security issues require a private report
under [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not a public support request.

Use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/gwf/x2c/issues) for bug reports,
documentation problems, and ideas. For bugs, include the output of
`x2c --version`, the platform, and a small reproduction where possible.
Submissions do not imply a response or resolution commitment.

There is no separate public discussion forum.
